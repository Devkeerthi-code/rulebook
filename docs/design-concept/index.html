01  The shelf

Every PDF your college ever sent, on one shelf.

Each upload becomes a cover with its type, effective date and page count, so you can see at a glance what Rulebook is reading from. Newer circulars wear a sticker.

    02  How it works

    Four steps from a 200-page PDF to one clear line.

    Admins upload once. Students ask forever. Nothing is answered from the internet or from memory, only from what is on the shelf.

    1. 1upload

      The admin drops in the PDFs

      Handbook, regulations, syllabi, placement policy, the circular from last Tuesday. Scanned copies too.

    2. 2we read it

      Rulebook reads every page

      Text is pulled out page by page, scanned pages go through OCR, and each clause is indexed with its page number.

    3. 3ask

      Students ask in plain words

      "Can I sit placements with one backlog?" No need to know which document or which section it lives in.

    4. 4get the page

      The answer opens to the page

      Every claim has a chip. Tap it and the PDF opens at that page with the passage highlighted. Check it yourself.

    03  The ask screen

    Calm on purpose. You came here to read a rule.

    The app itself drops the theatrics: a reading-size answer, citation chips, a clear warning when two documents disagree, and an honest "not in your documents" when it is not.

          06  Design system

          Paper, ink, one red pencil.

          A neutral paper-and-ink base, one vermilion accent for actions, citations and highlights. Yellow, red and green appear only when they mean something. Document types are told apart by pattern, never by colour.

          Signature interactions

          • Shelf drag & flickPointer-tracked velocity, then free glide with exponential friction. Past either edge the shelf stretches like a rubber band and springs home.friction 0.94/frame · rubber 0.35 · edge spring k=170 c=26
          • Idle driftWhen nobody touches it, the shelf drifts sideways like covers in a shop window. Stops on hover, focus, or reduced motion.12 px/s · resumes after 3 s idle
          • Cover leanCovers lean against the direction of travel, proportional to velocity, then settle upright.lean = clamp(−v / 300 px/s, ±7°) · eased 10/s
          • Sticker slapStickers scale in from zero with a stiff, underdamped spring and land with a small overshoot.k=420 c=16 m=1 (ζ≈0.39)
          • Square button pressPressing collapses the hard shadow and squashes to 94%; release overshoots back.k=600 c=22 (ζ≈0.45)
          • Heading revealWords rise out of a clip mask, 70 ms apart. The easing is a real spring, compiled into CSS linear() at load.k=170 c=19 → linear(), 900 ms
          • Bottom sheet (mobile)Drag the PDF sheet down; release past 30% or a flick faster than 0.6 px/ms dismisses it, otherwise it springs back.k=380 c=34 · flick 0.6 px/ms
          • App UIAsking, streaming and reading stay quick and quiet.all transitions ≤ 160 ms
